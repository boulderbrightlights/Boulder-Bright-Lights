Our Story

Best Friends Since 8th Grade, Business Partners Since Freshman Year

Boulder Bright Lights was founded by Ari and Aidan, Boulder natives and CU Boulder business students. We became best friends in 8th grade, and later roommates during our freshman year at the Leeds School of Business, where we combined our shared ambition for entrepreneurship to create our first company.

We're responsible, detail-oriented, and driven to succeed, not only in business, but in delivering an experience our neighbors can trust. By choosing us, you're supporting local students who take pride in every project and treat your home as if it were our parents'.
