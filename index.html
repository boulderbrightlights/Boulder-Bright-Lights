Our Promise

Make Your Home the Talk of the Neighborhood

Boulder Bright Lights is here to turn your Christmas light installation dreams into reality. Our dedicated local team helps families, neighborhoods, and businesses across the Front Range create captivating, custom holiday displays, starting with a personal design consultation to bring your vision to life with creativity and precision.

We use only top-tier, commercial-grade warm LED bulbs and heavy-duty socket wire, ensuring a bright, durable display that stands up to Colorado's harsh winters. Every installation is safe, efficient, and tailored to the unique character of your home or business.

What We Do

A Fully Managed Holiday Experience

Design Consultation

Professional Installation
